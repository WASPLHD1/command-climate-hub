Warrior Toughness

This section summarizes the Navy’s Warrior Toughness program.

Official source: MyNavyHR Warrior Toughness

Overview

Warrior Toughness strengthens mind, body, and spirit so Sailors can perform under pressure and recover quickly.

Program Basics

WT replaces E‑OSC and is not a mental‑health program — it is a warfighter performance program.
